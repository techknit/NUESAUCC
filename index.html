About NUESA_UCC



NUESA_UCC

The Nigerian Universities Engineering Students’ Association, University of Calabar Chapter (NUESA-UCC) is the vibrant, unified umbrella body representing the next generation of technical innovators within the Faculty of Engineering and Technology (FET) at the University of Calabar (UNICAL). Guided by its amended NUESA-UCC Constitution, which firmly outlines its democratic framework, student welfare goals, and legislative processes, the chapter bridges complex classroom theories with real-world engineering environments. Under the dynamic leadership of the Profectus Administration led by President Sen. Eneji Paul Ekune, the chapter heavily emphasizes technical brilliance, academic support, and smooth professional integration across multiple disciplines—ranging from Civil and Mechanical to Computer Engineering. A hallmark of the association is its dedication to transitioning scholars into field professionals, celebrated through historic milestones like the official induction of graduating classes into the prestigious Nigerian Society of Engineers (NSE) at the school's Senate Chambers. Communication and student engagement remain stellar via the modern NUESA-UCC Media initiative launched by the Office of the Director of Information (led by Comr. Chilaka, Princewill Kelechi) to streamline information flow across the faculty. Balancing rigorous lab work with community spirit, the association actively rounds out the student experience by hosting networking events, end-of-semester social gatherings, and collaborations that foster a true family culture among Malabites and Malabresses in engineering.
