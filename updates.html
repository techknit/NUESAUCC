The leadership of the Nigerian Universities Engineering Students Association, UNICAL Chapter (NUESA-UCC) hereby brings to the attention of all students, faculty members, and esteemed supporters an important update regarding the official merchandise for Engineering Vertex Week 2026. Following a thorough procurement review with our production partners, the planning committee resolved that quality, longevity, and superior comfort must take precedence. To ensure our members receive premium-grade fabric with durable, high-definition prints befitting the standard of Calabar Engineers, a slight upward adjustment has been made to the production specification. Consequently, the price for the official Engineering Vertex Week 2026 T-shirt has been revised from ₦6,000 to ₦6,500 across all available colorways (Black, White, and Green). We regret any inconvenience the initial pricing communication may have caused. We remain firmly committed to delivering merchandise of exceptional value and durability that truly represents the excellence of our association.
